Privacy Policy

Effective September 30, 2026

This policy describes Project 4 Classic, including its optional Project4 Cloud Sync connection to Google Drive, and this public website. Gosh Apps develops the app. Google sign-in is optional; local project work does not require a Google account.

Data the desktop app handles

The app stores the projects and settings you create or open on your computer. Project data can include task names and notes, resource information, calendars, scheduling and cost data, baselines, customizations, templates, macros, and attachments.

When you connect Google Drive, the app receives your Google account identifier and email address to identify the connected account and keep its sync library separate from other accounts. It also receives OAuth access and refresh tokens to make authorized requests to Google.

Google permissions and purpose

PermissionWhy the app requests it
openid and emailIdentify the Google account connected to your local library and display its email address.
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdataRead and write this app’s hidden application data in your Google Drive so your computers can sync the same library.

The Drive permission is limited to application data. The app does not request permission to browse your general Drive files, Gmail, contacts, or Google Calendar.

What sync uploads and downloads

Connecting enrolls your current project, except an unchanged demonstration project. Projects you subsequently create or open are automatically enrolled. The demonstration project is enrolled after you edit it. Sync includes enrolled projects and their notes, embedded attachments, managed copies of linked attachments, linked subprojects and resource pools, portable preferences, customizations, and the last-open workspace.

The app sends this data directly from your computer to Google Drive and downloads it directly to your other connected computers. Gosh Apps does not operate a server that receives your synced library. Machine-specific window placement, monitor layout, printer selection, and authentication tokens are not included in the synced library.

Storage and security

Local files, cached projects, and pending uploads are stored in your operating system’s per-user application directory. Flatpak uses the app’s sandbox directory. Imported original project files change only when you explicitly save them.

Google requests use HTTPS. Sign-in opens Google in your browser and returns to a temporary callback on your computer. The app uses the operating system’s protected credential storage when available. If protected storage is unavailable or denied, tokens remain in memory for the app session and you must sign in again after restarting; the app does not silently save plaintext tokens.

Project and attachment files are not end-to-end encrypted by the app. Local project contents are ordinary files protected by your operating system’s access controls. Google controls storage and protection within its service.

Use and sharing

Google account information and Drive data are used to provide account connection, synchronization, offline access, conflict recovery, and related features you use in the app. Gosh Apps does not sell this data, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models. The app does not include advertising or analytics tracking.

The app’s use of Google API data follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Your chosen Google account receives the synced data. Gosh Apps staff do not receive your project contents through the sync connection.

Retention, disconnection, and deletion

Local data and pending uploads remain on your computer until you remove them. Sync retains revision history and competing copies for recovery; older cloud revisions are not automatically deleted.

Tools → Cloud Sync → Disconnect stops syncing on that computer and removes its saved sign-in credentials. It leaves local and cloud data in place and does not disconnect your other computers.

You can revoke the app’s access through your Google Account connections. Google Drive also lets you remove hidden application data through its app-management settings. Disconnect all your computers before removing cloud data to avoid a connected device uploading its local library again. To remove local data, close the app and remove its managed library from each computer; separately saved project files remain wherever you saved them.

This website and support

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. It has no sign-in, forms, analytics scripts, or embedded third-party content. GitHub may process connection and request information as the hosting provider under its Privacy Statement. Google’s handling of your account and Drive storage is covered by Google’s Privacy Policy.

If you contact us through the linked GitHub support tracker, your submitted text and GitHub profile are visible in that public tracker. Share only what you intend to make public. Do not submit passwords, OAuth tokens, client secrets, confidential projects, or personal attachments.

Changes and contact

Updates to this policy will appear here with a revised effective date. Changes that require new Google permissions will require your consent before the app accesses that additional data. For questions or privacy requests, use the Support page. We can explain how to remove your local and Google-held data; we do not hold a separate server copy of your sync library.
