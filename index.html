Optional personal Google Drive sync

Project4 Cloud Sync is Project 4 Classic’s Google Drive connection. Sign in through your browser to keep projects, notes, attachments, preferences, and customizations together across your computers.

Changes save locally first and upload when a connection is available. Sync uses the app’s hidden data folder in your Google Drive. It does not request access to your general Drive files.

Read the Privacy Policy for the data and permissions involved, or visit Support for connection and recovery guidance.
